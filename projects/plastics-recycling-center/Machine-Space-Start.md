### Moveability
  A big issue we came across is moving parts from point A to point B. 
  Moving small things like plastic shavings and bottles is easy, but moving a shredder that is made out of solid metal will prove to have issues.

  While discussing I brought up the idea of using a table from the robotics lab to fix this issue.
